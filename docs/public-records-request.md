# Public records request: Palm Beach County Tax Collector

Send through the Tax Collector's public records request page (pbctax.gov → Help & Resources → Public Record Request).

---

**Subject:** Public records request: delinquent real property tax roll and tax certificate data (electronic format)

Under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, I request copies of the following public records in electronic, machine-readable format (CSV, Excel, or a database export), not as scanned images or PDF:

1. The **delinquent real property tax list** advertised for the most recent tax certificate sale, including for each account: parcel control number, owner name, tax year, and amount advertised.
2. **All outstanding (unredeemed) real property tax certificates**, both county-held and individually held, including: parcel control number, certificate number, sale year, tax year, face amount, interest rate, and redemption status. Certificate holder names may be omitted if they are exempt or confidential.
3. **Currently unpaid real property tax bills** (tax years delinquent but not yet sold), including: parcel control number, tax year, and amount due.

If any portion is exempt, please cite the statutory exemption and provide the remaining records. If there is a cost for extensive use of information technology resources under s. 119.07(4)(d), please send an estimate before fulfilling the request.
